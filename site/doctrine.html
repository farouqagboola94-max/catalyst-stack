§00 · Definition

On the word
“mafia”

Plainly: this has nothing to do with crime. The word is borrowed in the sense the technology world has used it for twenty years — the PayPal Mafia, the Stripe Mafia — meaning a small, loyal, disproportionately productive circle of operators who keep ownership inside the house, back each other without a contract, and leave alumni everywhere.

Lagos already produces that kind of circle. It has simply never written the method down. This is the attempt.

The Lagos Mafia Strategy is therefore a doctrine of concentrated ownership and asymmetric leverage: a way for one person, or a very small house, to produce at the volume of an institution — without borrowing the institution's permission, its capital, or its taste.

It is

An operating doctrine. A production method. A standard of ownership. A school you can join by using it.

It is not

A criminal metaphor, an exclusive club, a hustle-culture slogan, or a claim that hardship is a business strategy.

§01 · The Constitution

The five laws

Five laws govern what gets built. Take any one away and the other four stop compounding — which is the point of writing them as a constitution rather than a list of tips.

Law 01 · Ownership

Own the source

“Never rent the universe you are building. If someone else can revoke it, it was never yours.”

In full

Law by law

Each law is stated as an axiom, then broken into four parts: what it means, how it is executed, what happens when it is ignored, and which piece of standing work proves it.

01Ownership

Own the source#

“Never rent the universe you are building. If someone else can revoke it, it was never yours.”

Definition

Every layer that carries identity — story, characters, mythology, domain, archive — must be held outright. Platforms may be used for distribution. They may never be used for custody.

Mechanism

Original IP over licensed IP. Self-hosted sites over rented profiles. Public repositories over private drives. Source files kept beside every published output.

Failure mode

A decade of work living inside an account someone else controls. One policy change, one ban, one shutdown, and the body of work evaporates with nothing to rebuild from.

Proof

Catalyst: The Awakening — an original universe, not a licence. The characters, the Aṣẹ system, the art and the site are all held at the source.

02Leverage

Leverage over labour#

“One operator, a fleet of machines. Never do by hand what can be done by a standing instrument.”

Definition

Human hours go into judgement, taste and direction. Everything repeatable — generation, transcription, scaffolding, mirroring, deployment — is handed to tooling that runs whether or not I am awake.

Mechanism

Agent frameworks and coding agents for build work. Generation engines for image, video and audio. Scheduled actions for maintenance. Every manual task performed twice becomes a script.

Failure mode

Trading time for output at a fixed rate. Ambition then scales linearly with exhaustion, and the work stops the moment the operator does.

Proof

Catalyst Stack mirrors 300 repositories across 23 categories on a monthly schedule — a standing instrument that stocks the workshop by itself.

03Evidence

Ship in public#

“The archive is the proof. Nothing counts until a stranger can open it.”

Definition

Work is published as it stabilises, not when it is perfect. A public trail of commits, issues and live pages replaces the credential I was never going to be handed.

Mechanism

Public repositories by default. Live URLs over screenshots. Written method over private know-how. Free access to the canon so it can travel without a gate.

Failure mode

A hard drive full of unreleased brilliance. Invisible work compounds nothing — no audience, no feedback, no leverage, no record that any of it happened.

Proof

Eleven public repositories, a live comic universe with issues free to read, and this doctrine itself — written down where it can be argued with.

04Position

Local blood, global reach#

“Be specific enough to be from somewhere. Be legible enough to travel anywhere.”

Definition

Lagos is not a constraint to apologise for — it is the source of the material. The details stay exact: Mushin, danfo dust, three-in-the-morning current. The craft stays world-standard so nothing needs translating.

Mechanism

Yoruba cosmology carried accurately rather than decoratively. Nigerian specificity in the writing. International production values in the typography, the art and the ship quality.

Failure mode

Two ways to lose: sanding off the origin until the work is generic, or leaning on the origin so hard that it becomes a novelty act. Both trade the thing that made it worth reading.

Proof

A Mushin teenager carrying the Aṣẹ of the Orishas — unmistakably Lagos, and readable by anyone in the world who has ever felt invisible.

05Compounding

Compound the stack#

“Every project must lower the cost of the next one. If it does not, it is a hobby.”

Definition

Projects are not a portfolio of unrelated attempts. Each one is required to leave behind an asset — a tool, a pipeline, an audience, a reusable body of knowledge — that the next one inherits.

Mechanism

Generation engines feed the comic art. Transcription and agent tooling feed production. The fork archive feeds the tooling. The canon feeds the audience that receives all of it.

Failure mode

Ten years of starting over. Impressive individual efforts that share no substrate, so the eleventh project is exactly as expensive as the first.

Proof

The work index reads as one system: generation, transcription and agent tooling all terminate in the canon they were assembled to produce.

§02 · Execution

The operating loop

The laws say what is true. The loop says what to do on a Tuesday. Five stages, run continuously, in order, forever.

Stage 01

Source

Decide what is worth owning. Reject anything that cannot be held outright or does not belong to one of the four domains.

Stage 02

Forge

Build it with instruments, not hours. If a step is done twice by hand, it becomes a script before it is done a third time.

Stage 03

Ship

Publish at the point of usefulness, not the point of perfection. A live URL, a public repository, a readable issue.

Stage 04

Archive

Record the method beside the output. What was learned is stored so it is never re-learned at full price.

Stage 05

Compound

Ask what the next project inherits from this one. If the answer is nothing, the loop was run badly. Return to Stage 01.

§04 · Conduct

Rules of engagement

The laws in their working clothes — short enough to check a decision against in under ten seconds.

  1. If a platform can delete it, it is not an asset — it is a rental. Keep the master.
  2. Do it by hand once to learn it. Automate it before the third time.
  3. Publish at eighty per cent. The last twenty arrives faster with an audience attached.
  4. Never make the origin an excuse or a costume. Make it the material.
  5. Every project must name the asset it leaves behind before it is started.
  6. Read the source of any tool you depend on. Dependency without understanding is a debt.
  7. Keep the circle small and the work open. Loyalty inward, output outward.
  8. Credit lineage precisely — the mythology, the forks, the people. Precision is respect.
  9. Speed is a feature until it costs ownership. Then it is a trap.
  10. Build for the child in Mushin who has never seen themselves in it. That is the audience of record.

§05 · Instrumentation

How it is measured

A doctrine that cannot be scored is a mood. These are the only five numbers that decide whether the strategy is working.

Ownership ratio

Share of the body of work held outright versus held on someone else's platform. Target: rising, permanently.

Leverage ratio

Output produced per operator-hour. Every new instrument should move this. If it does not, the instrument was decoration.

Public surface

Count of things a stranger can open right now — live pages, readable issues, public repositories. Private work scores zero.

Inheritance

For each finished project: what did the next one start with that it would otherwise have built? Nothing is a failing grade.

Reach without dilution

Audience growth measured against how much of the origin had to be sanded off to get it. Growth bought with specificity is a loss.

The override

If a decision satisfies four metrics and breaks Law 01, it is refused. Ownership is not tradeable against convenience.

§06 · Standing

Who this is for

Written for the operator who has more ambition than budget, more taste than permission, and no intention of waiting to be discovered.

It is for anyone building from a place the industry has decided is peripheral — and who has worked out that the periphery is only a distribution problem, and distribution is now solvable from a laptop.

The strategy is not a secret. It is written here in full so that it spreads. A mafia that cannot recruit is just a small room — and The Catalyst School is the recruiting.
