Orientation

What this place is

This is not a portfolio in the usual sense. A portfolio shows finished things. This shows the machine that finishes them — and it is deliberately open, because the method is the point.

I write and own a comic universe. I fork, wire and run the infrastructure that produces it. I keep an archive of everything worth keeping, and I publish from Lagos on purpose. Those four activities are not separate careers — they are one system, and each one lowers the cost of the next.

The system has a name. I call it the Lagos Mafia Strategy — five laws, one loop, four layers. It is written down in full on this site so that it can be argued with, copied, and used. And it is taught in the open, because a mafia that cannot recruit is just a small room.

The Method

The Lagos
Mafia Strategy

A mafia in the sense the tech world already uses the word — a small, loyal, unreasonably productive circle that keeps ownership inside the house. Not a syndicate. A school, and a way of moving.

Five laws govern what gets built. One loop governs how. Four layers govern what it is built on. It is written to be precise enough to follow on a bad day.

Read all five laws
  1. Own the source — never rent the universe you are building.
  2. Leverage over labour — one operator, a fleet of agents.
  3. Ship in public — the archive is the proof.
  4. Local blood, global reach — Mushin-specific, planet-legible.
  5. Compound the stack — every project must feed the next.

The School

For the next
sovereign generation

Nigerian youth are not short on talent — we are short on structure. The Catalyst School teaches the frameworks, tools and posture that turn brilliant hustle into an asset that compounds — and lifts the creative economy one operator at a time.

Four ways in: a free eight-week apprenticeship (The Interns · 5 lanes), one-to-one mentorship (The Circle), a quarterly cohort (The Forge), and the self-serve Catalyst OS Starter Pack.
