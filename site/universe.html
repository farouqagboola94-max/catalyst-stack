The Premise

A boy from Mushin
carrying every god

Adebayo “Bayo” Adeyemi is nineteen. He never asked for power. He carries the Aṣẹ of all the Orishas at once — Ṣàngó, Ògún, Ọ̀ṣun, Oya, Obatàlá, Ẹṣù — in a city that has never once made room for him.

This is not borrowed mythology, and it is not a Western hero dropped into African clothing. The Orishas are carried accurately. The Aṣẹ is a system with rules. The danfo dust, the three-in-the-morning current crackling through old Mushin streets, the ancient war that never ended — all of it is specific on purpose.

The Orishas chose their vessels before the vessels understood what choice meant.

Creator’s Declaration

This is not a comic.
This is a legacy.

I built this universe for the young ones coming — for every child in Mushin who looks at the sky and wonders whether someone who looks like them can save the world. For every Lagos street kid who has never seen their power reflected in a hero. For my lineage, and theirs.

We will not stop until every arc is told. Every villain faces their reckoning. Every Orisha gets their justice. Every child who ever felt invisible sees themselves in Bayo Adeyemi and knows: Aṣẹ flows in us all.
